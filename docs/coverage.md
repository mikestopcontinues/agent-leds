Columns: Start (Started), Prompt (Prompted), Perm (PermissionAsked), Quest (QuestionAsked), Answ (Answered), End (TurnEnded), End? (TurnEndedAsking), Fail (TurnFailed), Intr (Interrupted), Bg (Backgrounded), BgDone (BackgroundDone), Ended (Ended), Look (Looked), RdExp (ReadExpired), Aband (Abandoned), Dism (Dismissed).

One record can tell several signals, each a fact of its own. The first of them in this order with a rule from the session's phase is the move: Ended, Interrupted, TurnFailed, PermissionAsked, QuestionAsked, Answered, TurnEndedAsking, Backgrounded, BackgroundDone, TurnEnded, Prompted, Started, Dismissed, Abandoned, ReadExpired, Looked.

Signals of one family are told together by sound sessions:

- TurnEnded, TurnEndedAsking, Backgrounded, TurnFailed, Interrupted
- Prompted, PermissionAsked, QuestionAsked

Any other signals told together overlap: the order above still decides, and the daemon counts each set in `agent-leds status --json` and `agent-leds doctor`.

## Moves each harness cannot make

**PermissionAsked**: an approval wait shows as the light it interrupts.

- Cursor: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead, Failed → Blocked
- Junie: Idle, Working, Asked, AskedRead, Stopped, StoppedRead, Failed → Blocked
- Pi: Idle, Working, Asked, AskedRead, Stopped, StoppedRead, Failed → Blocked

**QuestionAsked**: a question the agent waits on shows as the light it interrupts.

- Cursor: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead, Failed → Blocked
- Junie: Idle, Working, Asked, AskedRead, Stopped, StoppedRead, Failed → Blocked

**Answered**: an answered approval or question shows as a question until the turn ends.

- Codex: Blocked → Working
- Grok: Blocked → Working
- Pi: Blocked → Working

**TurnEndedAsking**: a turn that ends on a question shows as stopped, not as a question.

- Cursor: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead, Failed → Asked

**TurnFailed**: a turn that ends on an error never shows the error light.

- Codex: Idle, Working, Background, Blocked, Asked, AskedRead, Stopped, StoppedRead → Failed

**Interrupted**: an interrupted turn keeps its light until the next turn or the process ends.

- Junie: Working, Asked, AskedRead, Stopped, StoppedRead, Failed → Idle

**Backgrounded**: a turn whose background work runs on shows as stopped while that work runs.

- Grok: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead → Background
- Cursor: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead → Background
- Junie: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead → Background
- OpenCode: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead → Background
- Pi: Idle, Working, Stopped, StoppedRead → Background

**BackgroundDone**: background work that finishes is never announced, and shows as working until the next turn.

- Codex: Background → Stopped

## Phases never entered

Searched from `Gone` over the signals a harness declares and the daemon's own.

- Codex: Failed
- Grok: Background
- Cursor: Background, Blocked, Asked, AskedRead
- Junie: Background, Blocked
- OpenCode: Background
- Pi: Background

## Stuck phases

Phases with a light that a harness reaches but leaves only when its process ends or through the daemon's own signals. Each is a place where an experiment or a deliberate guess is worth considering.

No harness has a stuck phase.
